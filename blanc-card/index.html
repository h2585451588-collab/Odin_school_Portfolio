I · About the Game

Touch.
Imagine.
Tell.

Blanc Card is a tactile storytelling game built around touch and imagination.

In this game, instead of looking at the cards, players use their hands to explore illustrations made from raised dots on the card. Each player decides what the shapes might represent and uses that idea to build a story based on their Theme Card.

Based on different players the same card can inspire completely different ideas, so every story develops in its own way.

II · Theme Cards

Choose a world.

At the beginning of the game, each player draws one card from the Theme Deck. The card provides the theme for that player’s story.

III · Blanc Cards

Feel the picture.

Blanc Cards have no words or printed pictures. Each card contains an abstract pattern made from raised dots in the front of the cards.

Players feel the pattern and imagine what it could be. One player might interpret a card as a dragon, while another might imagine a mountain, a river, or something entirely different, anything is possible!

IV · How to Play

One card at a time.

Draw your cards

Each player draws one Theme Card and one Blanc Card.

Begin your story

Players have five minutes to explore their Blanc Card and create the opening of a story based on their theme.

Share with the group

Players take turns sharing the beginning of their stories.

Draw another Blanc Card

At the beginning of each new round, every player draws another Blanc Card and uses it to continue their story.

Players may exchange Blanc Cards during a round if both players agree.

Finish the story

Play continues for four rounds. In the final round, each player completes their story and shares the ending with the group.
